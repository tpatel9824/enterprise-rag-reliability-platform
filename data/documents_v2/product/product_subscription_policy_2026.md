# Subscription and Cancellation Policy

## Plan Changes

Monthly customers may change plans at the next billing boundary. Annual subscriptions normally remain active through the committed term. A downgrade does not erase charges already incurred, and account access continues until the applicable end date.

## Cancellation

An authorized account administrator may submit a cancellation request through the account portal or Support. Support verifies the requester and records the effective date. Cancellation stops future renewal but does not automatically create a refund.

## Related Decisions

Refund eligibility is governed by the current Customer Refund Policy and any signed customer terms. Support follows the Support Refund Procedure before representing that money will be returned. Contract-specific commitments take precedence over this general policy when the contract is explicit.

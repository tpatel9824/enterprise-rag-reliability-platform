# Customer Refund Policy

## Eligibility

Customers may request a refund within 14 calendar days of the original charge when the signed agreement does not specify different terms. Usage-based fees and completed professional services are non-refundable. Cancellation by itself does not create a refund; eligibility must be evaluated under this policy and the customer agreement.

## Approval Workflow

Support verifies the account, charge, contract type, and stated reason before creating a refund request. Support must not promise a refund before approval. Finance approval is required for refunds above five thousand dollars, disputed invoices, or exceptions to the standard eligibility rules.

## Processing Time

AcmeFlow initiates an approved card refund within three business days. The customer's bank may take another five to ten business days to display the credit. Support should distinguish AcmeFlow's initiation time from the bank's posting time when communicating an estimate.

## Policy Precedence

Signed customer terms take precedence when they explicitly modify eligibility or timing. This version supersedes Customer Refund Policy version 3.0 effective January 1, 2026. Historical version 3.0 remains available only for questions about transactions governed by the earlier effective period.

# Customer Refund Policy

## Eligibility

Customers could request a refund within 30 calendar days of the original charge. Usage-based fees and completed professional services were not refundable. Support had to confirm the account, charge, and purchase channel before opening a request.

## Approval

Support documented the reason and sent eligible requests to Finance. Support could explain the process but could not promise approval. Finance made the final decision when the request exceeded five thousand dollars or involved a disputed invoice.

## Timing

Approved card refunds were initiated within five business days. A bank could require an additional seven to fourteen business days to display the credit. This policy was superseded by version 4.0 effective January 1, 2026.

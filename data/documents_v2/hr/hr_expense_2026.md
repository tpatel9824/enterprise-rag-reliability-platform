# Employee Expense and Travel Policy

## Allowable Expenses

Expenses must be reasonable, business-related, and supported by an itemized receipt. Personal purchases, commuting costs, and avoidable penalties are not reimbursable. A missing-receipt declaration may be accepted only when the employee explains why the original cannot be obtained.

## Pre-approval

Travel expected to exceed two thousand dollars requires manager approval before booking. A flight above twelve hundred dollars also requires pre-approval unless an emergency exception is documented. International travel should be submitted early enough for security and insurance review.

## Submission and Payment

Employees should submit complete reimbursement requests within 30 days of the expense. Finance normally processes a complete request within 10 business days. An incomplete request is returned for correction, and the processing period begins again when the required evidence is supplied.

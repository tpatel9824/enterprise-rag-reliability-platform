# Support Operations Procedure

## Refund Intake

Support verifies the requester, account, charge, contract type, and stated reason before opening a refund request. The agent links the relevant invoice and records whether the customer has already cancelled. Cancellation does not establish refund eligibility.

### Customer Commitments

The agent checks the current Customer Refund Policy and signed customer terms. Support may describe review steps and published timing but must not promise approval. Approved exceptions and refunds above five thousand dollars require Finance authorization.

## Technical Escalation

Widespread service failure, repeated authentication failure, or confirmed data loss is escalated to Engineering with impact, timestamps, and reproduction details. A suspected credential compromise or unauthorized access is escalated immediately to Security. Support must not independently investigate a customer's systems or expose internal incident evidence.

## Customer Communication

Updates distinguish confirmed facts from estimates and use the cadence assigned by the incident commander. Contract-specific response commitments override the standard support target. Sensitive security details are shared only through approved channels and with authorized recipients.

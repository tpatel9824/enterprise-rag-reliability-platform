# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

## Eligibility

Eligible employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval. Roles requiring laboratory, facilities, or customer-site access may require more on-site attendance. Managers apply the policy consistently while considering documented role requirements.

## Working Practices

Employees maintain agreed working hours, attend required meetings, and keep their work location current. Remote work is not a substitute for dependent care during scheduled working time. Temporary arrangements lasting more than four weeks require Human Resources review.

## Information Security

Company data must be accessed from a company-managed device using approved authentication. Employees must avoid public display of confidential material and report a lost device immediately under the Security Incident Standard. Production access remains subject to the Access Control Standard regardless of work location.

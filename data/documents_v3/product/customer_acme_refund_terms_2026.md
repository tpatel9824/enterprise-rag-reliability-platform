# Customer Acme Signed Refund Terms

## Applicability

These signed terms apply only to Customer Acme transactions from April 1 through December 31, 2026. They modify the standard refund policy only for that customer and effective period.

## Refund Window

Customer Acme may request a refund within 21 calendar days of the original charge. AcmeFlow initiates an approved card refund within two business days.

## Unmodified Terms

Requirements not explicitly modified here remain governed by the standard Customer Refund Policy. Support must verify the customer account before applying these terms.

## Policy / Guidance

The AcmeFlow work week is Monday through Friday. Standard full-time employees work 40 hours per week unless an employment agreement states otherwise. Employees must complete annual compliance training by the assigned deadline. Managers approve schedules, time-off requests, and policy acknowledgements.
